Privacy

Privacy & POPIA notice

How this website handles personal information.

Think Mybiz Tech respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Responsible party

Think Mybiz Tech, 48 Rabe Street, Mokopane, Limpopo. Information Officer: MF Banda — info@thinkmybiztech.com, 063 621 5642.

What we collect

When you contact us, request a project, open a client account or place an order, we receive the details you choose to give us — such as your name, company, email address, phone number and project information. When you submit the enquiry form we also record the date and time and a one-way coded version of your internet address, used only to prevent spam and abuse. Orders are processed through Hostinger Ecommerce's hosted checkout. If you open a client account we also keep your account details, project requests, messages, quotations, invoices and payment records, and a log of important actions (such as accepting a quotation) for security and audit purposes.

Where it is kept

Enquiries and client-account records are stored in a private area and database on our website hosting and sent to our business mailbox. Passwords are stored only in a one-way encrypted (hashed) form. Our website, email and store are hosted by Hostinger, which processes information on our behalf as an operator; its servers may be located outside South Africa. If you contact us on WhatsApp, the conversation is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations and proposals, deliver services you have ordered, issue invoices, record payments and meet our legal obligations, including tax record-keeping. We send marketing messages only if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to respond, prepare and manage any resulting engagement, and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations. Invoices and payment records are kept for at least five years as required by South African tax law. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Cookies and tracking

Essential cookies keep you logged in to the Client Hub. We remember which campaign or website referred you (for example a Facebook advert) so we can see which marketing works; this is stored in your own browser and attached only to requests you submit. Advertising cookies from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn and TikTok are used only if you click "Accept" on the cookie notice. They let these platforms measure our adverts and may involve transfer of information to those companies outside South Africa under their own privacy policies. You can change your choice at any time: reset cookie preferences. Your browser also stores your shopping cart locally.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing, by emailing info@thinkmybiztech.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Client systems

Data held in applications we build or host for clients belongs to those clients. Each client's data is kept strictly separate and handled under the terms of its service agreement.
