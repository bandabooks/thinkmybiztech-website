Our difference

Compliance is where we start

Most software companies add compliance at the end. We start with it. Our team's roots are in professional accounting, tax and compliance work — so whether we build a payroll system, a fleet tracker or a community registration app, the data it produces can be relied on by management, auditors and regulators.

About Think Mybiz Tech
  • Accounting integrity — balanced double-entry postings, subledgers and reconciliations.
  • Tax awareness — VAT, PAYE, UIF, SDL and income tax treatment designed in.
  • Audit trails — who did what, when, and with whose approval.
  • Data protection — POPIA-aligned handling of personal information and consent.
  • Governance — role-based permissions and approval workflows.
  • Reliable reporting — one source of truth for every figure.
Our platform

Taxnify — a Think Mybiz Tech product

Taxnify is our proprietary business and compliance operating system for South African businesses: accounting, tax, compliance, payroll, point of sale and business operations. It is available to any business as a subscription — and it is the proven foundation our engineers build client applications on, so every custom solution starts with secure sign-in, permissions, audit trails and financial accuracy already in place.
