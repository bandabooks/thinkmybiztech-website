About us

Compliance professionals who build software

Think Mybiz Tech designs, builds, hosts and manages systems that keep a business's records, people and processes accurate, secure and audit-ready.

Who we are

A South African software engineering and business automation company

Our team's roots are in professional accounting, tax and compliance work. That experience shapes everything we build: we understand what business owners, auditors and regulators expect from a system, and we design for it from the first day.

We turn business requirements into usable software — from discovery and solution design to implementation, hosting and long-term support. We are not limited to one product or one industry, and every system is delivered as a modern application that works on every device.

Vision

Every business, whatever its size or industry, runs on systems that are automated, compliant and audit-ready.

Mission

To turn manual, paper-based and spreadsheet processes into secure, mobile-ready applications — built with the discipline of compliance professionals and the speed of a modern development team.
